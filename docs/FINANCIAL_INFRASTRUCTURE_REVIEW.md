# Can Open Collective + Wise + PayPal Be the Financial Infrastructure for Veiled Dominion?

**Public review draft — not a financial/legal commitment**

We are designing the financial infrastructure for a creative ecosystem that has both community-facing activity and potentially proprietary commercial IP.

The current proposal separates three jobs:

- **Open Collective:** project-level financial activity, contributions, budgets, expenses, reimbursements, and transparency where appropriate.
- **Wise:** money movement, multi-currency receiving/holding/conversion, and bank-style payouts where appropriate.
- **PayPal:** customer-facing payment acceptance, invoices, payment links, and—where supported—an Open Collective payment/payout rail.
- **iCloud:** private administrative evidence such as contracts, invoices, rights records, tax/legal documents, and supporting documentation.

The question is not simply **“Which payment service should we use?”**

The real question is:

> **What kind of money is this, who legally owns it, what record should be authoritative, and which payment rail should move it?**

### Questions for review

1. Where should community contributions live?
2. Where should sponsorships and grants be recorded?
3. Should proprietary commercial revenue ever pass through Open Collective?
4. When should PayPal be used directly rather than through Open Collective?
5. When does Wise add enough value to justify another financial account?
6. What should remain private in the administrative archive?
7. What should be publicly transparent?
8. What legal/entity structure should exist before the first significant transaction?
9. What accounting workflow would make reconciliation simple rather than creating another layer of bookkeeping?
10. What happens when the project operates in multiple currencies?

### Proposed principle

**Transaction layer ≠ payment rail ≠ administrative archive.**

A customer might pay through PayPal.  
The money might ultimately be held or converted through Wise.  
The project activity might be recorded in Open Collective.  
The contract and invoice might be retained in iCloud.

Those are four representations of one economic event, not four separate revenues.

### What we are deliberately not deciding yet

This proposal does not decide:

- the final legal entity;
- tax treatment;
- fiscal-host selection;
- ownership of commercial revenue;
- whether every category of Veiled Dominion activity belongs in Open Collective;
- final banking/payment accounts.

Those decisions should follow legal/accounting review and the actual operating model.
