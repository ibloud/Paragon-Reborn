# Naming and Trademark Status

## Designation

**PARAGON / PARAGON REBORN — INTERNAL WORKING NAME / NOT CLEARED FOR PUBLIC BRAND USE**

**Status: RESTRICTED — TRADEMARK / NAME CLEARANCE REQUIRED**

This designation is a project-control label. It is not a legal opinion and does not establish trademark rights.

## Current rule

Until a documented legal/name-clearance review authorizes otherwise:

- Do not present **PARAGON** or **PARAGON REBORN** as a cleared public-facing product, company, service, or game brand.
- Do not use `®` or imply federal registration.
- Do not make affiliation, sponsorship, endorsement, ownership, or trademark-clearance claims concerning third parties.
- Treat the current repository name, historical project references, and internal documentation as **working identifiers**, not evidence of trademark clearance.
- Public announcements should use a neutral project description or an approved replacement name unless legal review expressly clears the working name.
- Existing historical references may remain where needed for provenance, asset/licensing history, repository continuity, or technical context, but should not be converted into new branding claims.

## Why this gate exists

The project operates in fields that include games, software, AI, developer tooling, and security. The word **PARAGON** is used by third parties in related commercial fields. The exact phrase **PARAGON REBORN** should not be treated as cleared merely because an exact-match federal registration was not identified in a preliminary search.

The USPTO recommends searching confusingly similar marks, related goods/services, federal and pending marks, state records, common-law use, domains, and internet use before filing or adopting a mark. A comprehensive legal clearance is therefore a separate task from repository documentation.

## Evidence status

- **Exact-name screening:** preliminary.
- **PARAGON screening:** preliminary and materially crowded in relevant software/game/AI areas.
- **Common-law/state/international clearance:** not established by this repository document.
- **Attorney clearance:** not established.
- **Public-brand authorization:** **NOT ESTABLISHED**.

## Review boundary

Any change from this designation to a public-brand designation requires:

1. documented name/trademark clearance evidence;
2. identification of relevant goods/services;
3. review of confusingly similar marks and common-law uses;
4. explicit project-owner/legal approval; and
5. an auditable record of the decision.

**Do not infer clearance from absence of a search result.**

## Relationship to project architecture

The naming gate is part of the project's broader **Build Before You Build** principle: ownership, rights, evidence, provenance, and clearance are established before a name or asset is treated as a durable public-facing project identity.
