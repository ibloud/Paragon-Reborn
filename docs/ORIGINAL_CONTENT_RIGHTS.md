# Original-content transition and rights boundary

Date reviewed: 2026-10-08. Project policy for the new experiment; not a legal opinion or clearance certificate.

## Decision

Use original writing and generic placeholders for the next prototype. No Epic asset import is needed. Build independent characters and a distinct world from broad design interests: tactical teamwork, preparation, restraint and consequential choices. Historical inspiration is documented; homage is not permission to copy protected expression or use someone else's branding.

Do not export the archived Paragon tarot roster, its illustrations, lore or PDF into a new game, printed deck, merchandise or story generator as though it were cleared original content. Existing archive files are retained for provenance, not certified for distribution by this update. Audit them before further republication; the repository-wide license cannot grant third-party rights.

## Official sources checked

- [Fab Standard License summary and EULA](https://www.fab.com/eula): describes project incorporation and compatible-tool use; standalone redistribution is restricted. A general summary does not establish which terms govern a particular acquired asset.
- [Epic Content License Agreement](https://www.unrealengine.com/eula/content): distinguishes designated UE-Only content, limits source distribution and retains licensor ownership. It also restricts use of NoAI content in generative-AI inputs. Applicability depends on the actual acquisition and accepted terms.
- [Terra listing](https://www.fab.com/listings/5ea6bcb6-e43e-4bbe-813f-c19d8c907565) and [Greystone listing](https://www.fab.com/listings/122fd7bf-6f12-4304-a930-cccbbacdaebc): describe Unreal Engine packages, prohibit using PARAGON to name or advertise a game, and display “Allows usage with AI: No.” The retrieved license field was not populated. No asset acquisition entitlement was verified here.

Do not infer that every Fab asset is UE-only, that every historical character-name use is categorically prohibited, or that availability grants all character, story, trademark or embedded third-party rights. The new project independently chooses original identities and prohibits Epic character content in its runtime and generation inputs. Verify asset-specific terms if a later exception is proposed.

[Open PR #13](https://github.com/ibloud/Paragon-Reborn/pull/13) proposes a separate asset-integration contract. It is not merged policy merely because this document cites it. Reconcile both changes before any optional licensed-asset integration; this original-placeholder path does not depend on importing those assets.

## Content admission record

For every proposed release asset or text, record: stable ID; creator and contributor agreement; source URL or creation record; creation/acquisition date; license and version; permitted uses and attribution; modifications; third-party dependencies; AI restrictions; intended distribution; review status; and replacement/removal disposition. Unknown rights means not release-ready. Do not store private contracts in public repositories.

For new hero designs, review name, visual design, backstory, relationships, voice, equipment and distinctive ability expression together. A cosmetic rename does not establish independence. Working labels and the working game title have not received trademark clearance. Credit inspiration only in reviewed descriptive context, without implying endorsement or using PARAGON as the product identity.

Tarot concepts and suit labels do not clear a modern deck's art, guidebook prose, fonts or translations. This increment uses newly written descriptions and no reproduced tarot artwork or quotations. Any later public-domain claim needs an exact work, creator, publication date, jurisdiction and reliable source; commission original art or obtain an appropriate license instead of assuming all tarot material is free.

## Repository and publication controls

- Keep third-party binaries and restricted assets out of public source control and browser bundles.
- Do not use excluded assets as model inputs, training data, tracing references or prompts to produce close character substitutes.
- Keep original prototype data independent of historical roster IDs and names. Archive references must not be automatically ingested by story generation.
- Preserve each repository's existing license; linking a document does not relicense its contents. The engine repository's ShareAlike content terms must not be applied to Epic material.
- Before public release, review branding, provenance, contributor rights and exact distribution scope with qualified counsel where unresolved. No automatic publication/merchandising follows from a research document.
- Inventory remaining historical public pages, downloads, previews and repository descriptions before claiming migration complete. Retain appropriate attribution without promoting legacy branding.

The first milestone is an original design foundation. It is not a claim that all existing historical material has been legally reviewed or that the complete ecosystem is free of third-party dependencies.
