
# Legacy Project Validation

## Purpose

Return to the Void / Paragon ReBorn is being developed not only as a game-development sandbox, but as a controlled environment for learning how to bring older or previously paused project material up to current production standards.

That includes technical standards **and** rights, provenance, accessibility, data, documentation, and governance.

This is a validation framework, not a declaration that historical Paragon material is cleared for any particular use.

## Validation sequence

~~~
OLD / EXISTING PROJECT
        ↓
ARCHIVE + INVENTORY
        ↓
PROVENANCE AUDIT
        ↓
RIGHTS / LICENSE AUDIT
        ↓
DEPENDENCY AUDIT
        ↓
CURRENT-STANDARD GAP ANALYSIS
        ↓
CONTROLLED REBUILD
        ↓
PLAYABILITY / ACCESSIBILITY / DATA TEST
        ↓
DOCUMENTED RESULT
        ↓
PRODUCTION DECISION
~~~

## What Paragon ReBorn validates

The sandbox may be used to validate:

- gameplay and systems implementation;
- character rebuilding;
- technical art and presentation;
- animation and interaction;
- accessibility;
- telemetry and statistical balance analysis;
- aim-assist experiments;
- networking and trust boundaries;
- technical writing and provenance;
- playability-card evidence;
- contributor training and graduation;
- separation between third-party material and original work.

The validation environment must not silently turn a third-party dependency into project-owned IP.

## Rights boundary

Epic's released Paragon material is treated as a **candidate external asset source**, not as automatically cleared production content.

For every external asset or historical project element that may survive into a current build, record:

| Field | Question |
| --- | --- |
| Source | Where did it come from? |
| Provenance | What evidence establishes its origin? |
| License/version | Which current or historical terms apply? |
| Technical reuse | How is it used in the sandbox? |
| Modification | Is it modified? |
| Training | May it be used in the training environment? |
| Demo/case study | May it appear publicly? |
| Grant | May it appear in a grant submission/pitch? |
| Commercial | May it ship commercially? |
| Name/character/mark | Does public use depend on protected identity? |
| Redistribution | May it or a derivative be redistributed? |
| Production handoff | Can it move into another production project? |
| Replacement | What is the clean replacement path? |
| Evidence | Where is the supporting rights document? |
| Decision | Cleared / pending / restricted / replace / counsel review |

**Technical reuse is not legal clearance.**

No asset should be represented as cleared merely because it appears in an Epic release, a historical build, a repository reference, or a prototype.

## Rebrand boundary

The working project name may change.

The final public game identity must be evaluated separately from the technical asset pipeline. Do not assume that the ability to use an asset permits use of the original game's name, character names, trademarks, marketing language, or other identifying material.

A rebrand is therefore part of validation, not cosmetic cleanup.

## Legacy-project evidence

When an old project is brought forward, preserve:

- what was inherited;
- what was independently rebuilt;
- what was replaced;
- what remains unresolved;
- what changed because of current standards;
- what evidence supports each decision.

The case study should show corrections and rejected paths, not only the finished result.

## Relationship to training

The sandbox is a proving ground.

~~~
TRAINING
   ↓
CHARACTER REBUILD
   ↓
GAME-GRID PLAY
   ↓
PLAYABILITY CARD
   ↓
GRADUATION
   ↓
DOCUMENTED PRODUCTION CAPABILITY
   ↓
SEPARATE PRODUCTION ACCEPTANCE
~~~

Graduation demonstrates capability. It does not transfer third-party rights, make a character canonical, or guarantee production placement.

## Relationship to Veiled Dominion

Veiled Dominion is a separate production destination.

A sandbox character, mechanic, visual treatment, or narrative idea does **not** automatically enter Veiled Dominion.

The receiving production project decides what is accepted, modified, rejected, licensed, or made canonical.

## Data and AI boundary

Gameplay telemetry may be used for controlled statistical analysis, balancing, accessibility, and aim-assist research subject to applicable participant/data controls.

That is distinct from training or fine-tuning a generative AI system on participant creative work, voices, likenesses, unreleased material, or other protected content.

PIXIE may explain or contextualize observed evidence. PIXIE does not silently convert telemetry into claims about player intent or identity.

## Validation record

Use this lifecycle for discovered issues:

~~~
DISCOVERY → ASSUMPTION → VERIFICATION → CORRECTION → CONTROL → RETEST
~~~

A restriction discovered during validation is a successful finding if it prevents an unsafe or uncleared production decision.
